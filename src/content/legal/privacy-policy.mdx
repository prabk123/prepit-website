This policy explains what personal data PrepIt collects, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. PrepIt is run by DocWhizz Ltd, a company in England and Wales ("we", "us"). We are the controller of your personal data under UK and EU data protection law (UK GDPR and GDPR).

PrepIt is an iPhone app and this website. Android is coming soon; this policy will be updated before it launches.

## Summary

- We use your data to run PrepIt: your nutritionist chat, food log, meal plans, check-ins and reminders.
- Food, health and body data is sensitive. We only process it because you choose to give it to us, and you can delete it.
- We use AI model providers to answer your messages, read meal photos and voice notes, and build plans. They process that data on our behalf.
- We don't sell your data and we don't share your health data with advertisers.
- Apple handles your payment. We never see your card details.

## What we collect

### Data you give us

- **Account details:** your name and email address from Sign in with Apple or Google.
- **Profile and goals:** age, sex, height, weight, body measurements, activity level, goals, dietary preferences, allergies and the answers you give during your consultation and weekly check-ins.
- **What you log:** meals and drinks (typed, from photos, from Siri or the Action button, or from chat), water, supplements and weigh-ins.
- **Meal photos:** photos you take or choose so PrepIt can work out what you ate.
- **Voice notes and calls:** audio you record for the nutritionist, and the transcript we make from it. If you use a voice call with the nutritionist, the conversation is processed to answer you.
- **Chat history:** the messages you send to and receive from the nutritionist, including reactions and replies, and any reports or feedback you send us from the app.
- **Meal plans, recipes and grocery lists** you create or save, and meal plans you choose to share by link.

### Data from connected services (only if you turn them on)

- **Apple Health:** if you allow it, PrepIt reads data such as active and resting energy, steps, workouts, heart rate, weight and body composition, and can write the meals, nutrients and weigh-ins you log back to Apple Health. You can change this at any time in the Health app. Apple Health data is never used for advertising or marketing.
- **Strava:** if you connect it, we receive your activities to account for exercise.

### Data collected automatically

- **Device and usage data:** device model, iOS and app version, time zone, language, notification token, crash reports, and how you use the app (for example which screens you open and which features you use).
- **Subscription status:** whether you are in a free trial or subscribed, from Apple through our subscription provider. We don't receive your card details.
- **Advertising measurement:** only if you allow tracking when iOS asks, we share limited events (such as starting a trial) with Meta to measure our ads.

## AI processing

PrepIt's nutritionist, food recognition, transcription and meal planning use AI models run by third-party providers (such as OpenAI and Google, reached through OpenRouter, and Deepgram for speech). To answer you, we send them what the task needs, which can include your first name, goals, recent logs, meal photos, voice notes and messages. They process it on our behalf to produce a response.

AI answers can be wrong. PrepIt gives general nutrition guidance, not medical advice. See our [Terms and Conditions](/terms-and-conditions).

## Who we share data with

We share personal data only with providers that help us run PrepIt, under contracts that limit how they can use it:

- **Hosting, storage and email:** Railway (servers and database), Amazon Web Services (photos and voice notes), Google Firebase (sign-in, notifications and crash reports), Brevo and PostHog (emails).
- **AI and search:** OpenRouter and the model providers it connects to, Deepgram, Pinecone (food database search) and Tavily (web search for food nutrition information, which receives food names, not your account details).
- **Subscriptions:** Apple and RevenueCat.
- **Analytics and monitoring:** PostHog, Mixpanel, Google Analytics for Firebase and Axiom.
- **Advertising measurement:** Meta, only if you allow tracking.
- **People you choose:** if you share a meal plan link, anyone with the link can see that plan.
- **The law:** if we are legally required to, or to protect our users' safety.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising except as described above with your permission.

## International transfers

Some of our providers process data outside the UK and EU, including in the United States. Where they do, we rely on safeguards recognised by UK and EU law, such as the UK–US data bridge, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account in the app (Settings, then Account), it is scheduled for deletion and permanently deleted after a 30-day grace period, during which you can ask us to cancel the deletion. We may keep limited records for longer where the law requires it, for example for tax. Anonymous, aggregated usage statistics may be kept.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw consent. California residents have the right to know, delete and correct, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights.

To use any of these rights, email **support@tryprepit.app**. We will reply within one month. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

## Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit, access controls and providers with strong security practices. No system is perfectly secure, so please tell us at support@tryprepit.app if you think your account has been compromised.

## Children

PrepIt is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will tell you in the app or by email before they take effect. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
