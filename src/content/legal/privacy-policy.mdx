This policy explains what personal data PrepIt collects, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. PrepIt is run by DocWhizz Ltd, a company in England and Wales ("we", "us"). We are the controller of your personal data under UK and EU data protection law (UK GDPR and GDPR).

If you buy a subscription on our website, DOCWHIZZ, LLC, a Delaware company in the United States, sells it to you. It is also a controller of your purchase and billing details, such as your email address, plan, payment status and the country you bought from, and it follows this policy too.

PrepIt is an iPhone app and this website. Android is coming soon; this policy will be updated before it launches.

## Summary

- We use your data to run PrepIt: your nutritionist chat, food log, meal plans, check-ins and reminders.
- Food, health and body data is sensitive. We only process it because you choose to give it to us.
- We use AI model providers to answer your messages, read meal photos and voice notes, and build plans. They process that data on our behalf.
- We don't sell your data, and we don't share your food, health or body data with advertisers.
- Apple handles App Store payments. On our website, RevenueCat and Stripe handle payment for us. We never see your full card details.

## What we collect

### Data you give us

- **Account details:** your name and email address from Sign in with Apple or Google. Before you sign in, onboarding uses an anonymous account that is linked to your sign-in afterwards.
- **Profile and goals:** date of birth, sex, height, weight, body measurements, activity level, goals, dietary preferences, allergies and the answers you give during your consultation and weekly check-ins.
- **What you log:** meals and drinks (typed, from photos, from Siri or the Action button, or from chat), water, supplements and weigh-ins.
- **Meal photos:** photos you take or choose so PrepIt can work out what you ate.
- **Voice notes, dictation and calls:** audio you record for the nutritionist and the transcript we make from it, and voice calls with the nutritionist and their transcripts. When you dictate with the keyboard microphone, Apple's speech recognition turns your speech into text.
- **Chat history:** the messages you send to and receive from the nutritionist, including reactions and replies, and any reports or feedback you send us from the app.
- **Meal plans, recipes and grocery lists** you create or save, and meal plans you choose to share by link.

### Data from our website

- **Quiz answers:** your goal, what gets in your way, energy, hunger and mood, date of birth, sex, height, weight, goal weight, activity, exercise, diet, intolerances, how you like advice, your first name, and whether you're pregnant or breastfeeding, have had an eating disorder, or have a health condition that affects what you eat. Your answers stay in your browser until you save your plan by email or sign in. To work out your daily targets, the quiz sends your date of birth, sex, height, weight, goal, goal weight, activity and exercise to our servers, which don't keep them. If you're under 18 or tell us you've had an eating disorder, the quiz stops there and saves nothing to our servers.
- **Saving your plan by email:** if you give us your email at the end of the quiz, we save it with your answers, your country, the campaign that brought you to us, and whether you ticked the box for tips and offers (with the exact wording you saw). We email you a copy of your plan and a reminder the next day, and tips and offers only if you ticked the box. We don't send these emails to addresses that already have a PrepIt account.
- **Signing up on the website:** when you sign in with Apple or Google on our website, we save your quiz answers and the campaign that brought you to us to your sign-in, so the app can skip its setup.
- **Buying on the website:** your plan, trial and subscription status, from RevenueCat. Stripe collects your card details for RevenueCat; we never see them.
- **Waitlist:** if PrepIt isn't available in your country yet and you join the waitlist, your email address and country.
- **Contact form:** your name, email address and message.

### Data from Apple Health (only if you allow it)

PrepIt reads data such as active and resting energy, steps, distance, flights climbed, workouts and their routes, heart rate, resting heart rate, height, weight and body composition. It can write the meals, nutrients, water and weigh-ins (including body fat and lean mass) you log back to Apple Health. Energy and workout data is sent to our servers so the nutritionist can account for your activity. You can change access at any time in the Health app. Apple Health data is never used for advertising or marketing.

### Data collected automatically

- **Device and usage data:** device model, iOS and app version, time zone, language, notification token, crash reports, and the screens you open and features you use.
- **Session recordings:** for some sessions, our analytics provider (PostHog) records the app's screens, including the text and images on them, and the app's network requests, so we can see where people get stuck and fix bugs.
- **Subscription status:** whether you are in a free trial or subscribed, from Apple or our website checkout, through our subscription provider (RevenueCat). We don't receive your card details.
- **On our website:** the pages you visit and the steps of the sign-up you reach (never your quiz answers), through PostHog, and the campaign and ad click details in the link that brought you to us (such as utm_source or fbclid) and the site you came from. Once you sign in, these are linked to your PrepIt account. Session recordings on the website hide what you type and don't record the quiz or checkout. See our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy).

### Advertising measurement (Meta)

We use Meta's tools to measure whether our ads lead to installs and subscriptions:

- **In the app:** Meta's SDK receives app launches and in-app events, your PrepIt account ID and your email address (hashed on your device).
- **On our website:** the Meta Pixel receives the pages you visit, when you tap a link to the App Store, and that you saved your plan by email (without your email or answers), along with your IP address and browser details.
- **From our servers:** when you start a trial, subscribe or are charged, we send Meta the event with your hashed email address, IP address and device details.

In the app this happens whether or not you allow tracking when iOS asks. Your answer to that prompt tells Meta whether it may use the data to track you across other companies' apps and websites. On the website, it happens only after you accept optional cookies in the UK and EEA, and by default elsewhere. We never send Meta your food, health or body data, quiz answers, photos, voice notes or messages.

## AI processing

PrepIt's nutritionist, food recognition, transcription and meal planning use AI models run by third-party providers: Anthropic, OpenAI and Google (reached through OpenRouter), OpenAI directly for search embeddings, and Deepgram for speech. To answer you, we send them what the task needs. That can include your first name, age, sex, goals, latest weight, time zone, recent logs, Apple Health energy and workouts, meal photos, voice notes and messages. They process it on our behalf to produce a response.

When the nutritionist searches the web to answer a question, the search query (with personal details removed) goes to OpenRouter's web search.

AI answers can be wrong. PrepIt gives general nutrition guidance, not medical advice. See our [Terms and Conditions](/terms-and-conditions).

## Who we share data with

We share personal data only with providers that help us run PrepIt:

- **Hosting, storage and email:** Railway (servers and database), Amazon Web Services (photos and voice notes), Google Firebase (sign-in, notifications and crash reports), Brevo (sign-in emails), PostHog (product emails, notifications and the emails after the website quiz) and Vercel (this website).
- **AI and search:** OpenRouter and the model providers it connects to (including Anthropic, OpenAI and Google), OpenAI, Deepgram, Pinecone (food database search) and Tavily (web search for food nutrition information, which receives food names, not your account details).
- **Maps:** Mapbox, which receives workout routes to draw route maps.
- **Subscriptions and payments:** Apple for App Store purchases, and RevenueCat with Stripe for website purchases.
- **Analytics and monitoring:** PostHog (including session recordings), Mixpanel, Google Analytics for Firebase and Axiom (server logs).
- **Internal tools:** Slack, where our team receives problem reports you send from the app, account deletion notices (your name, email address and account ID), messages from our contact form (your name, email address and message) and waitlist sign-ups (your email address and country).
- **Advertising measurement:** Meta, as described above.
- **People you choose:** if you share a meal plan link, anyone with the link can see that plan.
- **The law:** if we are legally required to, or to protect our users' safety.

We do not sell your personal data.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. You can delete your account in the app: tap your profile picture, then **Account & Subscription**, then **Delete account**. Your account is closed straight away and scheduled for deletion. You have 30 days to change your mind by emailing support@tryprepit.app.

If you subscribed on our website, deleting your account turns off renewal, so you aren't charged again. We don't refund the rest of the period you've already paid for.

After those 30 days we delete your account and everything in it: your profile, settings, logs, meals and meal photos, plans, recipes, supplements, achievements, chat history, voice notes and calls, consultations and check-ins, the quiz answers you saved on our website, and your Apple or Google sign-in with PrepIt. Deletion runs in batches, so it can finish a little after the 30 days. If you'd like us to confirm it's done, email support@tryprepit.app. We may keep limited records for longer where the law requires it, for example for tax.

From our website:

- **Quiz answers in your browser** stay there until you sign in, and we stop using them after 30 days. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes them.
- **A plan saved by email** is deleted when you sign in to PrepIt (your answers move to your account), when you choose **Delete my details** from the link in any of those emails, or 30 days after you last saved it, whichever comes first. After **Delete my details**, we keep only a scrambled (hashed) copy of your email address, not the address itself, so we never email it from the quiz again. **Stop these emails** stops every follow-up straight away and keeps that scrambled copy too; the saved plan itself is then deleted on the same schedule as above.
- **Answers saved when you sign in on the website** are kept with your sign-in until you delete your account. If you signed in but never finished setting up, email support@tryprepit.app and we'll delete them.
- **Waitlist sign-ups and contact form messages** are kept until we've told you PrepIt has launched where you are, or answered you. Email us to have them removed sooner.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw consent. California residents have the right to know, delete and correct, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights.

To use any of these rights, email **support@tryprepit.app**. We will reply within one month. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

## US state privacy rights

If you live in a US state with its own privacy law, such as California, Washington, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia or Texas, you can ask to know, access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to get a copy of it. Some of these laws treat food, body and health data, including the health answers in our website quiz, as sensitive or consumer health data. We only collect it to provide PrepIt to you, with your consent, and we don't sell it or use it for advertising. To use these rights, email **support@tryprepit.app**. If we turn down your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer.

## Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit, access controls and providers with strong security practices. No system is perfectly secure, so please tell us at support@tryprepit.app if you think your account has been compromised.

## Children

PrepIt is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Our website quiz and checkout are for people aged 18 and over: if you tell the quiz you're under 18, it stops and saves nothing. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.
