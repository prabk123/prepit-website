This policy explains how PrepIt subscriptions work. PrepIt is an iPhone app, run by DocWhizz Ltd. Subscriptions are sold and billed through the Apple App Store. Android is coming soon.

If you bought PrepIt on the web through one of our checkout links, Stripe bills you instead of Apple. Where that changes something, this policy says so.

## Plans and prices

| Plan | Price (US) | Free trial |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Monthly | $14.99 a month | 7 days |
| Annual | $99.99 a year | 7 days |

The App Store shows the price in your local currency, including any taxes, before you confirm. Prices outside the US may differ.

## Free trial

- Both plans start with a 7-day free trial.
- Apple charges you for the plan you picked when the trial ends, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.
- Apple decides who is eligible for a trial. Usually it's one trial per Apple Account.

## Automatic renewal

Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each month or year, and Apple charges your Apple Account within 24 hours before the period ends. It keeps renewing until you cancel.

If we change the price, Apple will tell you in advance. For a price increase, Apple may ask you to agree to the new price before your subscription renews.

## How to cancel

On your iPhone:

1. Open **Settings** and tap your name.
2. Tap **Subscriptions**, then **PrepIt**.
3. Tap **Cancel Subscription**.

You can also cancel from the App Store app: tap your profile picture, then **Subscriptions**. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

If you bought on the web, open PrepIt, tap your profile picture, then **Account & Subscription**, then manage your subscription. This opens the Stripe billing page, where you can cancel.

## After you cancel

You keep full access until the end of the period you've paid for. After that, PrepIt's subscription features are locked until you subscribe again. Your account and data stay in place, so you can pick up where you left off. If you want your data deleted, delete your account in the app: tap your profile picture, then **Account & Subscription**, then **Delete account**.

## Payments and billing problems

Apple handles payments and billing for App Store subscriptions, and Stripe for web purchases. If a renewal payment fails, the payment provider may retry it and give you a short grace period to update your payment method. If payment still fails, your subscription ends.

## Refunds

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. See our [Refund Policy](/refund-policy).
