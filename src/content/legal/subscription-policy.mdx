This policy explains how PrepIt subscriptions work. PrepIt is an iPhone app, run by DocWhizz Ltd. Android is coming soon.

You can subscribe in two places, and the rules differ a little between them:

- **In the app,** through the Apple App Store. Apple sells and bills these subscriptions.
- **On our website** (tryprepit.app). We sell these subscriptions ourselves, through DOCWHIZZ, LLC, a Delaware company in the United States. RevenueCat runs the checkout and Stripe processes the card payment for us.

Either way, you get the same PrepIt and you sign in to the app with the same Apple or Google account.

## Plans and prices

| Plan | Price (US dollars) | Free trial |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Monthly | $14.99 a month | 7 days |
| Annual | $99.99 a year | 7 days |

On the website, prices are in US dollars and the checkout shows what you'll pay before you confirm. The App Store shows the price in your local currency, including any taxes, before you confirm. Prices outside the US may differ.

## Free trial

- Both plans start with a 7-day free trial. You pay nothing on the day you start.
- When the trial ends, you're charged for the plan you picked, unless you cancel before then. In the App Store, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. On the website, cancel any time before the trial ends.
- On the website, you get the free trial if you haven't had a PrepIt subscription or free trial before. If you have, the checkout shows that you'll be charged straight away. In the App Store, Apple decides who is eligible, usually one trial per Apple Account.

## Automatic renewal

Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each month or year and keeps renewing until you cancel.

- **App Store:** Apple charges your Apple Account within 24 hours before each period ends.
- **Website:** we charge the card you paid with at the start of each new period.

If we change the price, we'll tell you before it applies to you, and you can cancel before it does. For App Store subscriptions, Apple tells you and may ask you to agree to a price increase before your subscription renews.

## How to cancel

### If you subscribed in the App Store

On your iPhone:

1. Open **Settings** and tap your name.
2. Tap **Subscriptions**, then **PrepIt**.
3. Tap **Cancel Subscription**.

You can also cancel from the App Store app: tap your profile picture, then **Subscriptions**. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

### If you subscribed on our website

1. Go to [tryprepit.app/account](/account) and sign in with the same Apple or Google account you used to subscribe.
2. Tap **Manage subscription**. This opens your billing page.
3. Cancel there. You can also change your plan or update your card.

You can't cancel a website subscription in the app or through Apple. The app tells you where to go. If you subscribed through our older website checkout, before our current billing page, email **support@tryprepit.app** and we'll change or cancel it for you.

## After you cancel

You keep full access until the end of the period you've paid for. After that, PrepIt's subscription features are locked until you subscribe again. Your account and data stay in place, so you can pick up where you left off.

## Deleting your account

To delete your account, open PrepIt, tap your profile picture, then **Account & Subscription**, then **Delete account**.

- **Website subscriptions:** deleting your account stops future payments. We turn off renewal, so you aren't charged again, but we don't refund the rest of the period you've already paid for.
- **App Store subscriptions:** deleting your account does not cancel them. Cancel in your Apple Account settings, as above, or Apple will keep charging you.

## Payments and billing problems

Apple handles payments and billing for App Store subscriptions. For website subscriptions, RevenueCat and Stripe handle them for us, and we never see your full card details. If a renewal payment fails, the payment provider may retry it and give you a short time to update your card or payment method. If payment still fails, your subscription ends.

## Refunds

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. We handle refunds for website purchases. See our [Refund Policy](/refund-policy).
