PrepIt is an iPhone app sold through the Apple App Store. Apple processes every payment, so refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. PrepIt is run by DocWhizz Ltd.

## Free trial

Both plans start with a 7-day free trial. If you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, you won't be charged. See our [Subscription Policy](/subscription-policy) for how to cancel.

## How to ask for a refund

We can't issue refunds for App Store purchases ourselves. To ask Apple for one:

1. Go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) and sign in with your Apple Account, or use the "Report a Problem" link in your Apple receipt email.
2. Choose "Request a refund", then select PrepIt and the reason.

Apple decides every request under its own policy.

## Cancelling

Cancelling stops future renewals. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. We don't give partial refunds for unused time.

## UK and EU customers

If you live in the UK or EU, you may have a legal right to cancel within 14 days of buying. For App Store purchases you use this right through Apple, in the same way as above. Nothing in this policy affects your statutory rights.

## Need help?

If something isn't working, email **support@tryprepit.app** first. We'll try to fix it, and we can help you with a refund request to Apple.
