PrepIt is run by DocWhizz Ltd. You can subscribe in the app through the Apple App Store, or on our website (tryprepit.app) in the US. Who handles a refund depends on where you subscribed.

## Free trial

Both plans start with a 7-day free trial. If you cancel before the trial ends, you won't be charged. In the App Store, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. See our [Subscription Policy](/subscription-policy) for how to cancel.

## App Store purchases

Apple processes App Store payments, so refunds for them are handled by Apple, not by us. To ask Apple for one:

1. Go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) and sign in with your Apple Account, or use the "Report a Problem" link in your Apple receipt email.
2. Choose "Request a refund", then select PrepIt and the reason.

Apple decides every request under its own policy.

## Website purchases

We sell website subscriptions ourselves. RevenueCat runs the checkout and Stripe processes the card payment for us. To ask for a refund, email **support@tryprepit.app** from, or mentioning, the email address of the Apple or Google account you subscribed with. We decide website refunds case by case, and always give one where the law requires it. A refund goes back to the card you paid with.

## Cancelling

Cancelling stops future renewals. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. We don't give partial refunds for unused time.

Deleting your account stops future payments on a website subscription, but doesn't refund the period you've already paid for.

## UK and EU customers

If you live in the UK or EU, you may have a legal right to cancel within 14 days of buying. For App Store purchases you use this right through Apple, in the same way as above. Nothing in this policy affects your statutory rights.

## Need help?

If something isn't working, email **support@tryprepit.app** first. We'll try to fix it, and we can help you with a refund request to Apple.
