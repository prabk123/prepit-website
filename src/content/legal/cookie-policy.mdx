This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies used on the PrepIt website (tryprepit.app) and in the PrepIt iPhone app. PrepIt is run by DocWhizz Ltd. Our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) explains how we handle personal data more generally.

## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Apps use similar technologies, such as software development kits (SDKs) and on-device storage. We call all of these "cookies" in this policy.

## Cookies on our website

| Cookie | What it does | Type | How long |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| prepit-geo | Remembers whether you're in the UK or EEA, so we know whether to ask for consent | Strictly necessary | 1 year |
| prepit-analytics-consent | Remembers whether you accepted or rejected optional cookies | Strictly necessary | 1 year |
| PostHog (ph_*) | Measures how the site is used, including anonymised session recordings with form inputs hidden | Optional analytics | Up to 1 year |
| Meta Pixel (_fbp) | Measures whether our ads lead to App Store visits | Optional marketing | Up to 90 days |

Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights also measure page views and site speed. They don't use cookies or identify you.

## Your choices on the website

- **In the UK and EEA:** we ask before setting optional cookies. Nothing optional is set until you choose "Accept optional cookies". You can change your mind by clearing this site's cookies in your browser, which shows the banner again.
- **Elsewhere:** the optional analytics and marketing cookies are on by default. You can block or delete them in your browser settings, and the site will still work.

## In the app

The app uses on-device storage to keep you signed in, remember your settings, and save changes while you're offline. It also uses SDKs for crash reports, notifications, product analytics and session recordings, and subscriptions (Google Firebase, PostHog, Mixpanel and RevenueCat), and Meta's SDK to measure our ads.

Meta's SDK runs whether or not you allow tracking when iOS asks. Your answer tells Meta whether it may use the data to track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can change it at any time in iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. Our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) lists what the app sends to Meta.
