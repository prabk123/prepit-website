Your nutritionist knows your goals, your day and your last meal, so you can ask it anything and get an answer built on what you actually ate. You confirm every change before anything is saved.

## Start a chat

1. Tap **Ask Nutritionist** at the bottom of Home.
2. Type a question and tap send.
3. Your nutritionist replies in the chat.
4. Or tap the microphone to send a voice note. You get a reply you can listen to.

<Tip title="Log by chatting">“I had two eggs on toast an hour ago.” You confirm a card before anything is saved.</Tip>
<Tip title="Ask about your week">“How's my protein been?” or “show me a chart of my calories”.</Tip>
<Tip title="Check a menu or a product">Ask about a restaurant's menu or what's in something you're buying.</Tip>
<Tip title="Fix a log">“That pasta was actually Saturday” or “delete the cookie from yesterday”. You get a card to confirm first.</Tip>
</Tips>

## It doesn't wait for you to ask

Your nutritionist also speaks up when it matters: a check-in if you haven't logged by your usual time, a nudge when it spots a pattern like low protein, or a question when a meal is too vague to log. You can turn each kind of message on or off under Profile, then **Settings**.

<Note>It's a nutritionist, not a doctor. It gives nutrition guidance, not medical advice, and will tell you when something needs a professional.</Note>
