The fastest log is the one you don't have to open an app for. PrepIt works with Siri out of the box, and you can put a **Log a meal** button on the Action button, the Lock Screen or Control Center.

## Ask Siri

There is nothing to set up.

1. Say “Hey Siri, log a meal in PrepIt”.
2. When Siri asks what you ate, describe it the way you would type it, for example “porridge with a banana and a black coffee”.
3. When Siri asks when you had it, say “just now”, “an hour ago” or a time.
4. Your meal shows on Home as **Calculating…** and fills in by itself.

<Tips>
<Tip title="Skip the time question">Say “Log my lunch in PrepIt” or “Log dinner in PrepIt” and Siri only asks what you had.</Tip>
<Tip title="Say the food after Siri asks">Siri can't take the food in the same sentence as “log a meal”, so wait for the question.</Tip>
<Tip title="Also in Shortcuts and Spotlight">Log a meal is in the Shortcuts app and in Spotlight search too.</Tip>
<Tip title="Be specific">Brands, amounts and how it was cooked make the estimate closer. See [Describe meals for accurate logs](/guides/describe-meals-accurately).</Tip>
</Tips>

## Use the Action button

On an iPhone with an Action button, you can set it to run PrepIt's **Log a meal** Siri shortcut. Press and hold the button and Siri asks what you ate, without opening the app.

1. Open the Settings app and tap **Action Button**.
2. Swipe across to **Shortcut**, tap **Choose a Shortcut**, then search for PrepIt and pick **Log a meal**.
3. Press and hold the Action button to try it. When Siri asks what you ate, type or say it, then tap **Done**. Unlock your phone if asked.

<Note>**Prefer to open the app?** Pick **Controls** instead of Shortcut, then choose PrepIt's Log a meal. The Action button then opens PrepIt on Log Meal, ready to type or say what you ate. This needs iOS 18 or later.</Note>

## Add a Log a meal button to the Lock Screen

With iOS 18 or later, PrepIt's **Log a meal** control opens Log Meal for today with one tap.

1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, then tap **Customise**.
2. Tap the minus on one of the two buttons at the bottom to remove it.
3. Tap the plus in its place, search for PrepIt and pick **Log a meal**.
4. Tap **Done**.

## Or put it in Control Center

1. Swipe down from the top right corner to open Control Center, then tap the plus at the top left.
2. Tap **Add a Control**.
3. Search for PrepIt and pick **Log a meal**.
4. Log a meal is now in Control Center.

You'll find the same steps, with screenshots, in the app under Profile, then **Tips and tricks**.
