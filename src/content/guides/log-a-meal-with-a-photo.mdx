A photo is the quickest way to log a plate of food. PrepIt works out what's on it, and a few words from you fill in what a photo can't show.

## Log a photo

1. Tap **+**, then **Log Meal**, then tap the camera next to the microphone.
2. Choose **Take photo** or **Photo library**.
3. Pick your photo. Add a few words if you like, then pick when you ate.

<Tips title="For the best results">
<Tip title="Get the whole plate in">Shoot from above in good light, with nothing cut off.</Tip>
<Tip title="Add what the photo can't show">Oil, butter, sauces, sugar in drinks, or “I had half of this”.</Tip>
<Tip title="Name the brand or restaurant">A few words next to the photo make the estimate much closer.</Tip>
<Tip title="Fix it if it's off">Your photo meal counts straight away. Tap it on Home to change the amount or anything in it.</Tip>
</Tips>

## What you get back

Each photo becomes a normal meal in your Log: its calories, carbs, protein and fat, the items PrepIt found, and what the meal did to your Energy, Satiety and Mood.
