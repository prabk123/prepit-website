PrepIt's widgets put your day where you already look. Several of them have buttons that work right on the widget, so you can add a glass of water or tick off a supplement without opening the app.

## Which widget does what

- **Meal plan** shows your next planned meal with **Log it** and **Skip**, and moves on to the next meal when you tap one.
- **Today's plan** (large) lists every meal on today's plan, with Logged, Skipped, or Log it and Skip on each.
- **Today's Nutrition** and **Today's calories** show what you've eaten against your goals.
- **Energy, Satiety & Mood** shows today's three scores in rings coloured like the ones on Home.
- **Water** shows today's water against your goal, with buttons that add a drink straight away.
- **Supplements** lists today's supplements with a tick for each one.
- **Streak** shows your day streak and the next milestone.

<Note>The buttons on the Meal plan, Today's plan, Water and Supplements widgets work without opening PrepIt on iOS 17 or later. With no signal, the widget still updates and PrepIt saves the change the next time you open it.</Note>

## Add one to your Home Screen

1. Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
2. Tap **Edit** in the top corner, then choose **Add Widget**.
3. Search for PrepIt and pick it.
4. Choose the widget and size you like, then tap **Add Widget**.
5. Tap **Done**.

## Add one to your Lock Screen

1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, then tap **Customise**.
2. Tap the widget area under the time.
3. Pick PrepIt, then choose a widget.
4. Tap the widget to add it.
5. Tap **Done**.
