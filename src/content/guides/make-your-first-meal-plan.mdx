Your nutritionist can plan your week around your goals, what you like to eat and your budget, then help you stick to it.

## Start a plan

1. Tap **+**, then **New Meal Plan**. You can also ask your nutritionist to plan your week.
2. Answer a few quick questions, like which days to plan, and review the draft.
3. Your plan is on the **Plan** tab. Tap it to open it.

## Swap a meal

1. Open your plan and tap the three dots.
2. Tap **Edit**.
3. Tap the swap icon on the meal you want to change.
4. A chat opens about that meal. Ask for alternatives, pick one from the list, then confirm the **Update plan** card.

You can also just ask your nutritionist to swap a meal, and nothing changes until you confirm.

## Shop from one list

1. On the **Plan** tab, open your plan.
2. Tap **Grocery list**.
3. Tick items off as you shop.
4. Tap **Copy** or **Share** to send the list to someone.

Plans reuse the same staples across the week to keep the shop small. When you swap a meal, the list updates by itself.
