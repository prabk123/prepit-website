You don't search a database or weigh anything in PrepIt. You describe what you ate, the way you'd tell a friend, and PrepIt works out the rest from trustworthy sources like official brand and restaurant nutrition information. The more detail you give, the closer the estimate.

## Log a meal

1. On Home, tap **+**.
2. Tap **Log Meal**.
3. Describe what you ate, with sizes and brands, then tap the arrow.
4. Pick when you ate it and tap **Confirm**.
5. Your meal fills in on Home by itself.

## What makes a log accurate

</Examples>

## See what PrepIt assumed

Tap any meal on Home to see the items PrepIt found. Scroll down to **How it's calculated** to see how confident it is in the estimate and where the amounts came from. If something's off, change it there.
