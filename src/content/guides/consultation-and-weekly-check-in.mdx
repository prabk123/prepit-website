A good nutritionist starts by getting to know you, then checks in every week. PrepIt does the same, in short chats with your nutritionist.

## Your consultation

The consultation is three short chat sessions over your first week.

1. **Getting to know you.** About five minutes on why you're here, your main obstacle and what a normal day of eating looks like. Then you just eat normally and log it.
2. **What I've noticed so far.** Once you've logged about three days, your nutritionist shares what it noticed in your logs. You pick a focus for the weeks ahead, or two, then confirm with **Lock it in**. It also suggests two small swaps to try, based on meals you actually logged.
3. **Week in review.** About a week in, you look back at how the focus went and how your Energy, Satiety and Mood moved. This is your first weekly check-in.

Sessions 2 and 3 start from the consultation card on the **Progress** tab.

## Your weekly check-in

Each week your nutritionist looks back at what you logged, asks a few quick questions, like what felt easiest and what got in the way, and writes a report.

1. Open the **Progress** tab.
2. Tap **Weekly check-in**.
3. Read the report, then tap **Set as my focus**.

The report is framed around your own goal. It gives you a headline for the week, cards for calories, protein and logging, one key insight from your data, and one clear focus for the week ahead.

<Tips>
