Calories tell you what you ate. Energy, Satiety and Mood tell you how your food is likely to make you feel. They're three scores from 0 to 100, built from what you log alone. No wearable, no glucose monitor.

## What the three scores mean

- **Energy**: will you have steady energy and focus today? It's built from how close you are to your calorie goal, protein in the morning, sugar compared with other carbs, glycaemic load, and how you spread your meals through the day.
- **Satiety**: will you stay in control around food? It's driven by protein, fibre and how processed your meals are.
- **Mood**: how well did yesterday's food set you up for a calm, steady day?

Energy and Satiety update as you log today. Mood is built from yesterday's full log. Each ring on Home turns green, amber or red, so you can read your day at a glance.

## See what shaped a score

1. Tap a ring on Home.
2. See how that score was worked out for the day, piece by piece.
3. Back on Home, tap a meal in the Log.
4. Each meal has its own Energy, Satiety and Mood cards, with a tip for next time.
5. On Progress, tap **What your food did** to see what helped your week.
